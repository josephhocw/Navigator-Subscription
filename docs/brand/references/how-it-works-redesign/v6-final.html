How it works

This is the Navigator on a real chart

The RHO Navigator draws the floor, the ceiling and the middle line on your chart, then prints a plain buy or sell label the moment price reacts. Here is what that looks like on a trader's screen. Watch what it does at the floor, at the ceiling, and on a break.

Three setups the Navigator looks for

Pick a setup and watch it play out on the chart

Buy the rebound

Price falls to the support floor and reacts. The Navigator does not buy at the floor. It waits for price to rebound about halfway up to the middle line, then prints Buy. The stop sits just below support; the first target is the middle line, then resistance.

▼

Sell the pullback

Price rises to the resistance ceiling and reacts. The Navigator does not sell at the ceiling. It waits for price to pull back about halfway down to the middle line, then prints Sell. The stop sits just above resistance; the first target is the middle line, then support.

▲

Catch the breakout

Price pushes through the ceiling. The Navigator waits for a candle to close above resistance, then prints Breakout, so you join the move once it is confirmed instead of guessing at the line. The mirror is a breakdown: a sell after price closes below support.
