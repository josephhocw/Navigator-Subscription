"How it works" page — five redesigns, one recommendation

Five design agents each rebuilt the page from the same brief: real coloured candlesticks, the corrected buy/sell/breakout logic, examples moved up front, the "go deeper" overload section cut, and accurate proof added to entice subscribers. Open any live page below. My pick is at the top.

My pick

Build on v3 — setups-first bento, with three grafts

v3 wins on what you actually asked for. It gets to the trade examples immediately after a tight hero, draws them as accurate candlestick "trophy" cards (the Buy label sits above support, after the rebound — not at the floor), stays premium and modern, and holds up on a phone with no clicking required. It also reads light, which respects your "no information overload" note. It's the most complete page as-is.

The one thing it doesn't do as well as the runner-up is showcase. v2's "real trader screen" — watchlist, live chart, and a Telegram signal card side by side — is the single most enticing asset any of the five produced. So I'd graft that in rather than pick v2 whole (v2's tab interaction and desktop-only watchlist are a risk for an older, often-mobile audience). The plan:

  • Graft v2's trader-screen + Telegram signal card into v3's hero as a static showcase (no clicking needed, survives mobile) — this is the "I want that on my phone" moment.
  • Add the "it waits for the move, it doesn't catch the knife" line (from v4/v5) above the setups — it turns the corrected logic into the actual selling point.
  • Borrow a restrained slice of v5's brand polish (the settling-compass hero motif, light glass) — but keep the heavy blur dialled down so it doesn't choke older phones.

If you'd rather a single clean winner with no grafting: v3 as-is is the safe choice; v2 as-is is the bold one. Tell me which and I'll implement it on the live page.
