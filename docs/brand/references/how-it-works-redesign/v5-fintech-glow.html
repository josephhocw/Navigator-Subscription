What it is

A companion for your trading, not an autopilot

The Navigator is an indicator that runs on TradingView. It draws a channel around price: a ceiling where price tends to stop rising, a floor where it tends to bounce, and a middle line between the two. As the market moves, the channel redraws itself to keep up.

When price reacts at one of those levels, a label prints on the chart — buy, sell or breakout. You can see at a glance where the market sits in its range and which way the trend is leaning.

One thing it isn't: an automated trading system. The Navigator never places a trade for you. It shows you where the likely turning points are. You decide, and you place the trade.

The channel

Three lines that show you where price turns

Once you can see the ceiling and the floor, a chart stops looking random. Price tends to react at the three lines — and that is where the Navigator looks for trades.

Three setups

It waits for the move — it doesn't catch the knife

The Navigator never buys at the floor or sells at the ceiling. It waits for price to prove itself first: the bounce, the pullback, the break. That is what makes a signal worth taking.

Buy the rebound

Wait for the bounce off support

Price falls to the floor and reacts. You don't buy there. You wait for it to rebound about halfway up to the middle line — and the buy fires once the bounce is underway. Stop sits just below support; first target is the middle, then the ceiling.

Sell the pullback

Wait for the turn off resistance

Mirror image. Price rises to the ceiling and reacts. You don't sell there. You wait for it to pull back about halfway down to the middle line — and the sell fires there. Stop sits just above resistance; first target is the middle, then the floor.

Catch the breakout

Buy after price closes above the ceiling

Sometimes price pushes through and keeps going. The buy fires after a candle closes above resistance, not at the line itself. The mirror is a breakdown — a sell after price closes below support. Knowing the lines in advance is how you catch the move early instead of chasing it.

In every case the Navigator waits for confirmation before it prints a label. It is built to spot the turn, not to gamble on it.
