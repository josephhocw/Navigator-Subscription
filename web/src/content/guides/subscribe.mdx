Time to subscribe. Keep your two usernames from [Step 2](/guides/tradingview-and-telegram) handy — you'll enter both at checkout, and we use them to switch on your access.

## Pick your plan

- Go to the [pricing section](/#pricing) and choose your markets — a single market, a combo, or All Markets.
- One subscription per person, please. If you want more than one market, pick a combo or All Markets rather than subscribing twice — it's cheaper anyway.

<GuideShot maxHeight="460px" src="/guides/step-3/choose-your-markets.png" caption="Choose a single market, a combo, or All Markets." />

- Opened your Pepperstone account through us in [Step 1](/guides/open-pepperstone-free-tradingview)? Switch the toggle to **Pepperstone price** and pay less.

<GuideShot maxHeight="520px" src="/guides/step-3/pepperstone-price-toggle.png" caption="Switch the toggle above the plans to 'Trade With Us · Pay Less' for your lower price, then choose your markets." />

## Check out

- Click **Subscribe** on your plan.
- A quick pop-up asks about your Pepperstone account — pick the answer that matches you and continue to the payment page.
- Fill in your details carefully:
  - **TradingView username** — exactly as you wrote it down, capitals included.
  - **Telegram username** — without the @ sign.
  - Your email and name.
- Click **Subscribe** to pay.

<GuideShot maxHeight="460px" src="/guides/step-3/checkout-fields.png" caption="The payment page — your TradingView and Telegram usernames go in these two boxes." />

## Join your signal groups

Your welcome email arrives within a minute or two. If it's not there, check your spam folder and mark it "not spam" so the next ones land properly.

The email has three things for you to do — here's what each one is for:

<GuidePair src="/guides/step-3/welcome-email.png" caption="Your welcome email." aligns={['12%', '37%', '62%']}>

1. **Join the announcement channel** — guides, updates and resources land here.
2. **Join your markets** — tap the button for each of your markets to enter its private signal group, where the live signals arrive.
3. **TradingView access** — your Navigator switches on by **12:00 noon Singapore time the next day**, using the TradingView username you gave us (which is why the spelling matters).

</GuidePair>

A tip for the groups: each one has topics for different instruments. Press and hold any topic to mute it — most members mute the instruments they don't trade and keep the rest.

## Manage your subscription

It's all self-service — change or cancel whenever you like, no need to email us.

- Go to [the website](/) and click **Manage subscription** in the top menu.
- Enter the email you paid with — we'll send you a secure link.
- Open the link to **upgrade, downgrade, switch markets, update your card, or cancel**.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-3/manage-subscription-nav.png" caption="Manage subscription sits in the top menu of the website." />
