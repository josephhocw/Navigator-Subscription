## Pick your plan

1. Go to the [pricing page](/pricing) and choose your markets — a single market, a combo, or All Markets.
2. One subscription per person, please. If you want more than one market, pick a combo or All Markets rather than subscribing twice — it's cheaper anyway.
3. Opened your Pepperstone account through us in [Step 1](/guides/open-pepperstone-free-tradingview)? Switch the toggle to **Pepperstone price** and pay less.

## Check out

1. Click **Subscribe** on your plan.
2. A quick pop-up asks about your Pepperstone account — pick the answer that matches you and continue to the payment page.
3. Fill in your details carefully:
   - **TradingView username** — exactly as you wrote it down, capitals included.
   - **Telegram username** — without the @ sign.
   - Your email and name.
4. Click **Subscribe** to pay.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-3/checkout-fields.png" caption="The payment page — your TradingView and Telegram usernames go in these two boxes." />

## Join your signal groups

1. Your welcome email arrives within a couple of minutes. If you don't see it, check your spam folder and mark it "not spam" so the next ones land properly.
2. Click the buttons in the email to **join the Telegram group** for each of your markets — that's where the live signals arrive.
3. Your Navigator access on TradingView is switched on by **12:00 noon Singapore time the next day**. We'll use the TradingView username you gave us, which is why the spelling matters.

A tip for the groups: each one has topics for different instruments. Press and hold any topic to mute it — most members mute the instruments they don't trade and keep the rest.

<GuideCallout title="Changing things later">
You manage everything yourself from the website: click **Manage subscription** in the footer, enter the email you paid with, and open the link we send you. From there you can change plan, update your card, or cancel.
</GuideCallout>
