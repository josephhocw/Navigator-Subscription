Once your account is funded and you've placed your first trade, Pepperstone unlocks your free TradingView plan and sends it to you as a coupon link by email. Here's how to claim it — and how to keep it free after the first three months.

<GuideChecks title="What you'll need">
- Funded your Pepperstone account to your plan's level (See [the funding step in Step 3](/guides/trial/open-pepperstone-free-tradingview#add-funds))
- Placed your first trade (See [Step 4](/guides/trial/place-a-trade))
</GuideChecks>

## Watch for the coupon email

- Once you've deposited the amount for your plan and placed your first trade, Pepperstone emails you the link for your free three-month TradingView plan. It usually arrives within 24 hours.

<GuideShot narrow src="/guides/step-7/coupon-email.png" caption="The email from Pepperstone with your free TradingView coupon link." />

- The amount you deposit decides which plan you unlock:

| TradingView Plan | To Redeem | To Renew |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Essential** — worth $25 SGD/month | $400 SGD deposit + 1 trade | 12 lots |
| **Plus** — worth $50 SGD/month | $1,300 SGD deposit + 1 trade | 15 lots |
| **Premium** — worth $99 SGD/month | $3,900 SGD deposit + 1 trade | 35 lots |

- Already on a paid TradingView plan? Nothing's wasted — the three months are added on top of your current expiry date.

## Open the link and enter your card

- Click the link in the email. You'll be asked to fill in your card details — that's a normal part of switching the plan on.

## Why you'll see a $1 charge

- You'll notice a charge of $1, plus 9 cents of GST. This isn't a real payment.
- It's a temporary hold. TradingView — like most subscription sites — places it to check your card is valid and switch your plan on.
- Both the $1 and the 9 cents come back to your card within a few days, so in the end you pay nothing.

<GuideShot narrow src="/guides/step-7/dollar-charge.png" caption="On the checkout, your promo code brings it down to a $1 hold plus 9 cents GST — both refunded." />

## Keep it free every three months

<GuideCallout title="Your plan won't renew on its own">
When the three months are up, your TradingView plan simply ends — it won't auto-renew, and you won't be charged the full price. To carry on for free, renew it through Pepperstone before then by hitting your lot target below.
</GuideCallout>

- The free plan runs for three months at a time, and you can keep renewing it for free for as long as you like.
- All it takes is hitting your plan's lot target within each three-month window — the last column in the table above. For Essential, that's 12 lots.
- Different markets count differently towards that target:
  - **Forex & Gold** — 1 lot counts as 1
  - **Shares & Indices** — 10 lots count as 1
  - **Crypto** — 4 lots count as 1
- You can mix markets to get there — for example, 6 lots of Forex/Gold plus 60 lots of Indices makes 12 lots.

## Pepperstone emails you the new codes automatically

- You don't need to apply for the renewal. Once you hit your lot target, Pepperstone emails you a fresh coupon link for the next three months. Look out for a subject line like "You have reached a min of 35 lots traded" (the number depends on your plan).
- Click the link and redeem it the same way you did the first time, and you're set for another three months.

<GuideShot narrow src="/guides/step-7/renewal-email.png" caption="The renewal email from Pepperstone with your next coupon link — it arrives on its own once you hit your lot target." />
