Your access is switched on **within a few minutes** of signing up. Once it's on, the Navigator appears in your TradingView indicator list, ready to attach to any chart.

## Open your chart

- Log in at [tradingview.com](https://www.tradingview.com/).
- Click **Products** in the top menu, then **Supercharts**.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-4/open-supercharts.png" caption="Open the Products menu and choose Supercharts." />

## Attach the indicator

- Click **Indicators, metrics, and strategies** in the top panel.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-5/indicators-button.png" caption="Click Indicators in the top panel." maxHeight="155px" />

- Click on the **Invite-only** tab on the left and left click on **RHO Navigator [All Markets]**.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-5/invite-only-list.png" caption="Open the Invite-only tab and pick the RHO Navigator." maxHeight="300px" />

- It takes 15 to 20 seconds to load — the Navigator is doing its calculations. You'll see three dots at the top-left while it works.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-5/loading-dots.png" caption="The three dots at the top-left mean the Navigator is still loading." maxHeight="210px" />

- Once the indicator has finished loading, you should see the channel lines and labels appear, like this.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-5/navigator-attached.png" caption="The Navigator fully loaded — channel lines, labels and the trend panel." />

## If the channel lines don't show

- If you see labels but no channel lines (like the image below), scroll the chart to the left, or zoom out (hold **Ctrl** and scroll back on your mouse). The lines only draw once the channel's starting point is on screen — a TradingView quirk, not a fault.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-5/no-channel-lines.png" caption="Labels show but no channel lines yet — scroll left or zoom out to bring the channel's start into view." />
