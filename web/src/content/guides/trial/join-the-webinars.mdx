The webinar is on Zoom, usually on **Wednesday at 8pm** (Singapore time). The exact date and the Zoom link are posted in the Telegram group each week — tap the link a few minutes before it starts.

## What happens on the webinar

- Navigator training — how to read the channels and the buy and sell labels
- This week's trading opportunities across the markets in your plan
- Your questions, answered live

## Before your first webinar

Play around with the Navigator — but don't trade yet. We equip you with the trading skills at the webinar.

- Watch how the channels form on your charts
- Get familiar with TradingView — move around, zoom, switch instruments
- Try the Navigator on different timeframes
- At most, follow Robin's live calls if you want to trade
