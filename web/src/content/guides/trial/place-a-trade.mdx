Everything is connected. Here's how to place a trade without leaving the chart.

<GuideChecks title="What you'll need">
- Pepperstone connected to TradingView (See [Connect Pepperstone to TradingView](/guides/trading/place-and-manage-on-the-chart#connect-pepperstone-to-tradingview))
- Funds in your Pepperstone account (See [the funding step in Step 3](/guides/trial/open-pepperstone-free-tradingview#add-funds) on how to deposit)
</GuideChecks>

## On your computer

- **Check you're connected.** Your Pepperstone account name and balance show at the bottom of the chart. If not, see [Connect Pepperstone to TradingView](/guides/trading/place-and-manage-on-the-chart#connect-pepperstone-to-tradingview).

<GuideShot maxHeight="150px" src="/guides/step-6/account-connected.png" caption="Your connected Pepperstone account shows in the panel at the bottom of the chart." />

- Under **Symbol Search**, click the white **Pepperstone** icon.

<GuideShot maxHeight="240px" src="/guides/step-6/symbol-search-pepperstone.png" caption="In Symbol Search, click the white Pepperstone icon." />

- Now when you search for any instrument, they'll only show **Pepperstone** charts — and you can trade on them.

<GuideShot narrow src="/guides/step-6/pepperstone-only-results.png" caption="Now every result is a Pepperstone chart you can trade." />

- **Click Buy or Sell.** The trading panel opens on the right. Choose your order type: **Market** (buy or sell now), **Limit** (at your price or better), or **Stop** (once price passes your level).

<GuideShot narrow src="/guides/step-6/order-types.png" caption="The order-type tabs at the top of the trading panel: Market, Limit, or Stop." />

Then set the order up in the same panel, top to bottom:

## On your phone

- Same flow in the app: open the chart and make sure Pepperstone is connected.

- **Access the trading panel.**
  - **iPhone:** tap the **Buy/Sell** buttons at the top.
  - **Android:** tap the **two-arrows** symbol at the bottom.

The panel opens with the same fields as on computer — set your size, your take-profit and stop-loss, then tap **Buy** or **Sell** to place.
