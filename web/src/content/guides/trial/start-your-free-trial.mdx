Your trial gives you 10 days of the full All Markets plan — US, HK, SG and FXMC. Nothing is charged during the trial.

## Create your TradingView account (if you do not yet have one)

The Navigator runs on TradingView, so you need a TradingView username before you sign up.

- Go to [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/?aff_id=158252) and click **Get started for free**. Sign up with your email.
- Open the activation email TradingView sends you and confirm your account.
- Choose a username, and **write it down exactly as you typed it** — capital and small letters matter. You'll enter it at the checkout in a moment.

<GuideCallout title="Already have a TradingView account?">
You don't need a new one — you just need to know your exact username:

1. Log in to TradingView.
2. Click the round icon with a letter in the top-right corner.
3. Your username shows at the top of that menu. Write it down exactly, capitals included.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-2/tradingview-username.png" caption="Your username sits at the top of the profile menu." />
</GuideCallout>

## Open your trial link

<a class="btn btn-primary" href="https://buy.stripe.com/7sYeVf4CEfFNf6B7NS4ow0c?client_reference_id=drwealth-aug27">Start your free trial</a>

- Fill in your **email** and **mobile number**.
- Fill in your **TradingView username** and **Telegram username** (without the '@').
- Tap **Start trial**.

<GuideShot narrow maxHeight="500px" src="/guides/drwealth/trial-checkout.png" caption="The trial checkout page — fill in your details and tap Start trial." />

Your trial ends **Sunday 6 September, 11:59pm**. Your card is not charged before then, and you can cancel any time in 2 taps — see below.

## Check your welcome email

- The moment you sign up, a welcome email arrives with your setup instructions — the same steps you're following here.

<GuideShot narrow maxHeight="440px" src="/guides/drwealth/welcome-email.png" caption="The welcome email you receive straight after signing up." />

## Change or cancel anytime

You stay in control the whole trial, and we remind you on Telegram and by email before it ends.

1. Tap **Manage subscription** in any of our emails.
2. Enter your email — a secure link opens your page to cancel or switch plans.
