Two usernames matter here — write both down, you'll enter them when you subscribe.

## Create your TradingView account

- Go to [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/?aff_id=158252) and click **Get started for free**. Sign up with your email.
- Open the activation email TradingView sends you and confirm your account.
- Choose a username when asked, and **write it down exactly as you typed it** — capital and small letters matter.

<GuideCallout title="Already have a TradingView account?">
You don't need a new one — you just need to know your exact username:

1. Log in to TradingView.
2. Click the round icon with a letter in the top-right corner.
3. Your username shows at the top of that menu (for example, `JHOCW_trade`). Write it down exactly, capitals included.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-2/tradingview-username.png" caption="Your username sits at the top of the profile menu." />
</GuideCallout>

## Set your Telegram username

A quick word of warning: your Telegram **profile name** and your Telegram **username** are different things. The profile name is what friends see; the username starts life empty until you set one. We need the **username**.

### On Android

- Open Telegram and tap the three-line menu in the top-left.
- Tap **Settings**.
- Your username shows under your phone number. If it says "Username", tap it to create one.

<GuideShot narrow src="/guides/step-2/telegram-username-android.png" caption="Your username sits under your phone number in Settings." />

### On iPhone

- Open Telegram and tap **Settings** in the bottom-right.
- Tap **Edit** in the top-right.
- Tap the username field to see it, or to create one if it's empty.

<GuideShot narrow src="/guides/step-2/telegram-username-iphone.png" caption="The username row on the Edit screen — tap it to set yours." />

Write the username down without the @ sign.
