## Open a chart

1. Log in at [tradingview.com](https://www.tradingview.com/).
2. Click **Products** in the top menu, then **Supercharts**.

## Connect to Pepperstone

1. On the chart screen, click **Trade** at the top-right.
2. Find **Pepperstone** in the list of brokers and click **Connect**.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-4/connect-broker.png" caption="The Pepperstone tile in TradingView's broker list." />

## Approve the connection

1. Log in with your Pepperstone details and click **Approve Access**.
2. Done — your Pepperstone account name now shows at the top-right of the chart.
