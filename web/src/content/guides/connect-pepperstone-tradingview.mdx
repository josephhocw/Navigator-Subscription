This is the quickest step — about two minutes.

<GuideChecks title="What you'll get">
- Live prices on your charts
- Buy and Sell buttons that work straight from the chart
</GuideChecks>

## Open a chart

- Log in at [tradingview.com](https://www.tradingview.com/).
- Click **Products** in the top menu, then **Supercharts**.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-4/open-supercharts.png" caption="Open the Products menu and choose Supercharts." />

## Connect to Pepperstone

- On the chart screen, click **Trade** at the top-right.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-4/click-trade.png" caption="Click Trade at the top-right of the chart." maxHeight="340px" />

- Find **Pepperstone** in the list of brokers and click **Connect**.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-4/connect-broker.png" caption="The Pepperstone tile in TradingView's broker list." maxHeight="220px" />

## Approve the connection

- Log in with your Pepperstone details and click **Approve Access**.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-4/approve-access.png" caption="Confirm the account, then click Approve access." maxHeight="500px" />

- Done — your Pepperstone account name now shows at the top-right of the chart.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-4/account-connected.png" caption="Pepperstone is connected — your live account now shows on the chart." />

## Do the same on your phone (Optional)

- Download the **TradingView** app from the App Store or Play Store and log in.
- Open the chart and connect to Pepperstone.
