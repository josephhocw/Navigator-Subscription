## Create your Pepperstone account

- Sign up with your Apple, Google or Facebook account, or click **Continue with email** and use your email address.
- Use an email you check often — Pepperstone sends the verification result there.

## Create your profile

- Set **Country of residence** to **Singapore** and **Account type** to **Individual**, then click **Continue**.
- On the next page, check the country is still Singapore, tick **I agree to the terms and declaration**, then click **Continue**.

- Pepperstone now checks your details electronically. Most people are verified on the spot and land straight on the Trading Accounts page — if that's you, skip to [Are you verified?](#are-you-verified).

- Have your document ready: NRIC or driving licence (front and back), or your passport. Malaysia clients: IC front and back, plus driving licence front and back as proof of address.
- Choose your document type and follow the prompts. Click **Continue on phone** if you'd rather use your phone camera — it's usually easier. For the face scan, make sure you're the only person in the frame.

## Add your TradingView trading account

Everyone needs this step, new or existing. Your Pepperstone login is one thing; the trading account that connects to TradingView is added separately, once you're verified.

- On the **Trading Accounts** page, make sure the **Live** tab is selected, then click the blue **Add a trading account** button on the right.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-1/add-trading-account.png" caption="Trading Accounts, Live tab, then Add a trading account." />

- In the **Request New Live Account** box, set **Choose your preferred trading platform** to **TradingView** — that's the one that matters.
- Set **Currency** to **SGD** and leave **Account type** as **Razor**. Leverage is 1:200 on a Retail account; a Pro account defaults to 1:500. Add a nickname like "TV acc" if you like, then click **Submit**.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-1/request-tradingview-account.png" caption="TradingView as the platform, SGD, Razor, then Submit." />

- **One SGD account covers every market.** You trade every market straight from your Singapore dollar account. Your money is never swapped into the market's currency to buy and then swapped back; it stays in Singapore dollars throughout, so there's no need for a US dollar or any other foreign-currency account.
