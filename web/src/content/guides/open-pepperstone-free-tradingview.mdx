## Verify your identity

If a page appears showing an account number, your account is verified and you're done — skip ahead to "Tell us you've applied" below.

<GuideShot maxHeight="460px" src="/guides/step-1/account-verified.png" caption="An account number here means you're already verified." />

If Pepperstone asks for more verification, upload an ID document: NRIC or driving licence (front and back), or your passport. Then do the face scan — make sure you're the only person in the frame.

<GuideShot narrow src="/guides/step-1/verify-identity.png" caption="Verification is two steps: your ID document, then a face scan." />

If verification doesn't pass straight away, don't worry. Pepperstone reviews it by hand and emails you updates, and you can still add funds in the meantime. The usual causes are signing up with a different email or phone number than before, or a name or date of birth that doesn't match the ID.
