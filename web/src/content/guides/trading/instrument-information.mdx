- **TradingView shows you the lot size**, but not the rest. Leverage, spread, swap and commission don't appear on the chart.
- **Those live on your backend:** the broker platform your TradingView account is connected to. It's also your backup if TradingView ever has a problem (full story on the [backend page](/guides/trading/your-backend)).

## On Pepperstone

- **Log in to the Pepperstone portal and click Launch Webtrader.**

<GuideShot src="/guides/trading/instrument-information/pepperstone-launch-webtrader.png" caption="In the Pepperstone Secure Client Area, click Launch Webtrader." />

- **Type the instrument you want into the symbol search box** at the top of the chart panel. For example, Apple.

<GuideShot src="/guides/trading/instrument-information/pepperstone-symbol-search.png" caption="Type the instrument into the Symbol box at the top of the chart." />

- **Click the info icon and the information panel appears**, with every number for that market in one place.

<GuideShot src="/guides/trading/instrument-information/pepperstone-info-panel.png" caption="The AAPL.US information panel: contract size, margin rate, swap and commission, all together." />

Reading Apple (AAPL.US) off the panel:

- **Lot size (contract size) = 1** — one lot is one share.
- **Leverage = 1:5.** The panel shows a **margin rate of 20%**, not leverage. To convert, divide 100 by the margin rate: 100 ÷ 20 = **5**, giving 1:5.
- **Commission = 0.03 SGD per share**, per side.
- **Swap** — **long: a daily charge of 0.01697%**; **short: a daily credit of 0.00308%** (a negative figure is a charge, a positive one is a credit).

## On cTrader

cTrader calls it the symbol information, but it's the same idea: one screen with every number for that market.

1. Log in to cTrader.
2. In the symbol list or watchlist on the left, find the instrument you want.
3. Right-click the symbol and choose **Information** (it may be shown as **Symbol details**).
4. Read off the fields on the panel that opens.

<GuideShot src="/guides/trading/instrument-information/ctrader-symbol-info.png" caption="The cTrader symbol information panel for one instrument, showing the contract size, margin, swap and commission together." />

You're reading the same set of fields as on Pepperstone:

- **Contract size** — what one lot is worth in the market.
- **Margin** — how much of your money is held aside to open one lot. It's leverage looked at the other way round: a smaller margin means more leverage.
- **Swap** — the overnight adjustment, a charge or a credit.
- **Commission** — the small fee on forex and shares; nothing on an index.

<GuideCallout title="You'll never be guessing">
The fear here is "I won't know the rules for the thing I'm trading." But you do, whenever you want. Every number for any market is a few clicks away on your own platform, and because you're reading it live, it's never stale. Before you put real money on a market you haven't traded before, open its specification and have a quick look.
</GuideCallout>
