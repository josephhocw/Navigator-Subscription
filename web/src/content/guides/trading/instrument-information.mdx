Across the last few pages we've used numbers like leverage and the spread to explain how a trade works. Here's the useful part: you can look those up yourself, for any market, in a few clicks. You'll never have to take a figure on trust or wonder if it's gone out of date.

TradingView is your chart, but it doesn't show this information. The real numbers live on the platform behind it, your backend. That's the broker platform your TradingView account is connected to, and it's also your backup if TradingView ever has a problem (the full story is on the [backend page](/guides/trading/your-backend)). For now we just need it for one job: looking things up.

<GuidePlatformCheck />

The good news is that it's a single lookup. On the panel we're about to open, an instrument's leverage, lot size, spread, swap and commission all sit together on one screen.

<GuideInstrSpec />

## On Pepperstone

You'll open the instrument's specification, the screen that lists every number for that market in one place.

1. Log in to Pepperstone Webtrader and open a chart, or use the watchlist on the left.
2. Find the instrument you want, for example the Nasdaq 100.
3. Right-click the instrument and choose **Specification** (it may also be labelled **Information**).
4. Read off the fields, all on the one panel.

<GuideShot src="/guides/trading/instrument-information/pepperstone-spec.png" caption="The Pepperstone specification panel for one instrument, showing the leverage or margin, contract size, spread, swap and commission together." />

What each field is telling you:

- **Leverage, or margin requirement** — how much of your own money is held aside to open one lot. A bigger number here means less of your cash is tied up.
- **Contract size** — what one lot is worth in the market. This is what turns a price move into a dollar figure, the thing we worked through on the [lots page](/guides/trading/lots-and-what-its-worth).
- **Spread** — the small gap between the buy and sell price, the main cost of the trade.
- **Swap** — the small adjustment if you hold the trade overnight. It can be a charge or a credit.
- **Commission** — a small fee, and only on forex and shares. On an index it'll show nothing, just the spread.

## On cTrader

cTrader calls it the symbol information, but it's the same idea: one screen with every number for that market.

1. Log in to cTrader.
2. In the symbol list or watchlist on the left, find the instrument you want.
3. Right-click the symbol and choose **Information** (it may be shown as **Symbol details**).
4. Read off the fields on the panel that opens.

<GuideShot src="/guides/trading/instrument-information/ctrader-symbol-info.png" caption="The cTrader symbol information panel for one instrument, showing the contract size, margin, swap and commission together." />

You're reading the same set of fields as on Pepperstone:

- **Contract size** — what one lot is worth in the market.
- **Margin** — how much of your money is held aside to open one lot. It's leverage looked at the other way round: a smaller margin means more leverage.
- **Swap** — the overnight adjustment, a charge or a credit.
- **Commission** — the small fee on forex and shares; nothing on an index.

The fear here is "I won't know the rules for the thing I'm trading." But you do, whenever you want. Every number for any market is a few clicks away on your own platform, and because you're reading it live, it's never stale. Before you put real money on a market you haven't traded before, open its specification and have a quick look.
</GuideCallout>
