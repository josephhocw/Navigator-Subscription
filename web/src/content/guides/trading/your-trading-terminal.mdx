Your trading terminal sits behind your TradingView account — a full trading platform in its own right. It's where you look up a market's leverage and fees, see your full trade history, and manage your trades if TradingView is ever down.

<GuidePlatformCheck />

**Section A** below is the Pepperstone walkthrough. **Section B** is cTrader — if you opened your account before 29 April 2026, skip straight to it.

## On Pepperstone

Webtrader is your Pepperstone trading terminal — a full trading platform, your instrument information, your complete history, and your backup way in. Here's all of it, in order.

### Log in

- Log in to your **Pepperstone Secure Client Area** with your usual Pepperstone details.
- Click **Launch Webtrader** to open the trading platform in your browser. You'll see your chart, your account balance, and a panel along the bottom for your trades.

<GuideShot src="/guides/trading/instrument-information/pepperstone-launch-webtrader.png" caption="In the Pepperstone Secure Client Area, click Launch Webtrader." />

### Look up a market's information

TradingView shows you the lot size, but not the rest. The leverage, spread, swap and commission all live here, and you can pull them up for any market in a few clicks.

- **Type the market into the symbol search box** at the top of the chart panel. For example, Apple.

<GuideShot src="/guides/trading/instrument-information/pepperstone-symbol-search.png" caption="Type the instrument into the Symbol box at the top of the chart." />

- **Click the info icon, and the information panel appears**, with every number for that market in one place.

<GuideShot src="/guides/trading/instrument-information/pepperstone-info-panel.png" caption="The AAPL.US information panel: contract size, margin rate, swap and commission, all together." />

Reading Apple (AAPL.US) off the panel:

- **Lot size (contract size) = 1** — one lot is one share.
- **Leverage = 1:5.** The panel shows a **margin rate of 20%**, not leverage. To convert, divide 100 by the margin rate: 100 ÷ 20 = **5**, giving 1:5.
- **Commission = 0.03 SGD per share**, per side.
- **Swap** — **long: a daily charge of 0.01697%**; **short: a daily credit of 0.00308%** (a negative figure is a charge, a positive one is a credit).

### Place a trade

This is the same trade you'd place on the chart, done here on your trading terminal. Take it slowly and start small.

- **Click Buy or Sell.** The order ticket opens.
- **Choose your order type and set your trade size.** Pick **Market**, **Limit** or **Stop**, then set how much you want to trade.
- **Tick Protection Orders** if you want to set a take-profit and stop-loss.
- **Place it.** Click **Send Order**, and your open trade appears in the **Positions** tab at the bottom.

<GuideShot src="/guides/trading/pepperstone/place-order.png" caption="The Pepperstone order ticket: the Buy/Sell buttons up top, then order type and trade size, with Protection Orders for your take-profit and stop-loss." />

### Manage your open trades

- The bottom panel has tabs for **Positions** (trades open right now), **Orders** (trades waiting to trigger), and **Trade History** (everything you've closed).
- **Right-click any open position** to **close** it, or to **modify** it and change your take-profit or stop-loss.

<GuideShot src="/guides/trading/your-backend/pepperstone-webtrader.png" caption="Pepperstone Webtrader's bottom panel: the Positions, Orders, and Trade History tabs, with an open position showing its side, size, and live profit." />

### Use it on your phone

- You can do all of this from the **Pepperstone app** too. Download it from the **Google Play Store** (Android) or the **App Store** (iPhone), sign in with the same details, and the Positions, Orders and History tabs are all there.

## On cTrader

cTrader is your trading terminal — a full trading platform, your instrument information, your complete history, and your backup way in. Here's all of it, in order.

### Log in

cTrader has its **own password**, separate from your usual Pepperstone login. You set it once, then log in normally after that.

- **Open cTrader.** Go to [ct.pepperstone.com](https://ct.pepperstone.com) in your web browser and click **Log in** at the top right.

<GuideShot narrow src="/guides/trading/ctrader/login-button.png" caption="Go to ct.pepperstone.com and click Log in at the top right." />

- **Click Forgot password? (first time only).** On the login panel, click the **Forgot password?** link.

<GuideShot narrow src="/guides/trading/ctrader/forgot-password.png" caption="On the login panel, click Forgot password?." />

- **Reset your password.** Key in the email used for your Pepperstone account, tick **I'm not a robot**, and click **Reset password**. Check that inbox for the link to set your password.

<GuideShot narrow src="/guides/trading/ctrader/reset-password.png" caption="Enter your Pepperstone email and click Reset password." />

- After that, log in with your email and new password. Once you're in, you'll see your chart and the **Trade Watch** panel along the bottom for your trades.

### Look up a market's information

cTrader calls it the symbol information, but it's the same idea as anywhere: one screen with every number for that market.

- **Search for the market.** Click the symbol name at the **top left** of the chart, then type the market you want — for example, HK50.

<GuideShot src="/guides/trading/instrument-information/ctrader-symbol-search.png" caption="Click the symbol name at the top left of the chart to search for any market." />

- **Open Symbol info.** On the right-hand side, scroll down to the **Symbol info** panel. Every number for that market is listed there.

<GuideShot narrow src="/guides/trading/instrument-information/ctrader-symbol-info.png" caption="The cTrader Symbol info panel: lot size, commission, swap (long and short), and the trade-size limits, all in one place." />

You're reading the same set of fields you would on any platform:

- **Lot size** — what one lot is in this market (HK50 trades in lots of 1.0).
- **Commission (per lot)** — the fee to trade; an index like HK50 shows 0.00.
- **Swap (long) and Swap (short)** — cTrader quotes these as a **yearly** rate, so divide by 365 for the daily figure.
  - **Long:** -5.44% ÷ 365 ≈ -0.0149% a day.
  - **Short:** +0.44% ÷ 365 ≈ +0.0012% a day.
- **Min and max trade quantity** — the smallest and largest size you can put on.
- **Leverage** — scroll right to the **very bottom** of the panel for the **Leverage** section. HK50 is 1:100.

<GuideShot narrow src="/guides/trading/instrument-information/ctrader-leverage.png" caption="At the very bottom of the panel, the Leverage section shows the market's leverage — 1:100 for HK50." />

### Place a trade

This is the same trade you'd place on the chart, done here on your trading terminal. Take it slowly and start small.

- **Click Buy or Sell.** The order ticket opens.
- **Choose your order type and set your trade size.** Pick **Market**, **Limit**, **Stop** or **Stop-limit**, then set how much you want to trade.
- **Set your protection.** Tick **Stop loss** and **Take profit** to set where the trade closes. cTrader gives you more than the basics — see its [protection methods guide](https://help.ctrader.com/ctrader-web/trading/protections/):
  - **Trailing stop-loss** — a stop that follows the price as the trade moves into profit.
  - **Stop-loss to break-even** — moves your stop to your entry price once the trade is ahead, so a winner can't turn into a loss.
  - **Multiple take-profits** — up to 5 levels, so you can bank profit in stages.
- **Place it.** Click **Place order**, and your open trade appears under **Positions** in the Trade Watch panel.

<GuideShot narrow src="/guides/trading/ctrader/place-order.png" caption="The cTrader order ticket: order type, lot size, and the Stop loss, Take profit and break-even protections, with Place order at the bottom." />

### Manage your open trades

- Everything is in the **Trade Watch** panel along the bottom. Its tabs are **Positions** (open now), **Orders** (waiting to trigger), **History** (closed trades), **Price alerts**, and **Transactions** (money in and out). The bar along the bottom shows your account at a glance: **Balance**, **Equity** and **Free margin**.
- From an open position you can **modify** it to change your take-profit or stop-loss, **reverse** it (close it and open the opposite direction in one step), or **close** it.

<GuideShot src="/guides/trading/your-backend/ctrader-tradewatch.png" caption="The cTrader Trade Watch panel: the Positions, Orders, History, Price alerts and Transactions tabs, an open NAS100 position, and the account bar along the bottom." />

### Use it on your phone

- You can do all of this from the **cTrader app** too. Download it from the **Google Play Store** (Android) or the **App Store** (iPhone), sign in, and the Trade Watch tabs are all there.

If the screen ever breaks mid-trade, you're not stuck watching it. Log in here, find the position, and close or adjust it — the same trade, reached a different way, which is the whole point of knowing where it is before you ever need it.
