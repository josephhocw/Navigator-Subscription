Your trading terminal sits behind your TradingView account, and it's a full trading platform in its own right. It's where you look up a market's leverage and fees, find your complete trade history, reach a few tools the chart doesn't have, and — if TradingView is ever down — manage your trades from a second, separate way in.

Which one you're on depends on when you opened your account. Find yourself below, then open the walkthrough that's yours. Each one covers the same ground: logging in, looking up a market's information, placing a trade, managing your trades, and finding your full history.
