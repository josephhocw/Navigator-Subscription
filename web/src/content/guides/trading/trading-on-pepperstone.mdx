Webtrader is your Pepperstone trading terminal — a full trading platform, your instrument information, your complete history, and your backup way in. Here's all of it, in order.

## Log in

- Log in to your **Pepperstone Secure Client Area** with your usual Pepperstone details.
- Click **Launch Webtrader** to open the trading platform in your browser. You'll see your chart, your account balance, and a panel along the bottom for your trades.

<GuideShot src="/guides/trading/instrument-information/pepperstone-launch-webtrader.png" caption="In the Pepperstone Secure Client Area, click Launch Webtrader." />

## Look up a market's information

TradingView shows you the lot size, but not the rest. The leverage, spread, swap and commission all live here, and you can pull them up for any market in a few clicks.

- **Type the market into the symbol search box** at the top of the chart panel. For example, Apple.

<GuideShot src="/guides/trading/instrument-information/pepperstone-symbol-search.png" caption="Type the instrument into the Symbol box at the top of the chart." />

- **Click the info icon, and the information panel appears**, with every number for that market in one place.

<GuideShot src="/guides/trading/instrument-information/pepperstone-info-panel.png" caption="The AAPL.US information panel: contract size, margin rate, swap and commission, all together." />

Reading Apple (AAPL.US) off the panel:

- **Lot size (contract size) = 1** — one lot is one share.
- **Leverage = 1:5.** The panel shows a **margin rate of 20%**, not leverage. To convert, divide 100 by the margin rate: 100 ÷ 20 = **5**, giving 1:5.
- **Commission = 0.03 SGD per share**, per side.
- **Swap** — **long: a daily charge of 0.01697%**; **short: a daily credit of 0.00308%** (a negative figure is a charge, a positive one is a credit).

<GuideCallout title="You'll never be guessing">
The fear here is "I won't know the rules for the thing I'm trading." But you do, whenever you want. Every number for any market is a few clicks away on your own platform, and because you're reading it live, it's never stale. Before you put real money on a market you haven't traded before, open its information panel and have a quick look.
</GuideCallout>

## Place a trade

This is the same trade you'd place on the chart, done here on your trading terminal. Take it slowly and start small.

- **Open the order ticket.** With your market on screen, click **Buy** or **Sell** to open the order panel.
- **Choose the order type.** For your first trade, choose **Market** — it gets you in at the current price, no waiting. (Limit and stop are there for later.)
- **Set your size.** Start with the minimum lot. Not sure what that is? Clear the lot box and the panel shows you the smallest size allowed.
- **Set your protection before you place.** Set a take-profit (where the trade closes in profit) and a stop-loss (where it closes if the price turns against you). Decide both before you place, and your downside is set in advance.
- **Check you have the margin for it.** The panel shows the margin required against your free funds. If you've enough spare, you're good to go.
- **Place it.** Confirm the order, and your open trade appears in the **Positions** tab at the bottom.

<GuideShot src="/guides/trading/pepperstone/place-order.png" caption="The Pepperstone order ticket: order type, lot size, and your take-profit and stop-loss, all in one place." />

## Manage your open trades

- The bottom panel has tabs for **Positions** (trades open right now), **Orders** (trades waiting to trigger), and **Trade History** (everything you've closed).
- **Right-click any open position** to **close** it, or to **modify** it and change your take-profit or stop-loss.

<GuideShot src="/guides/trading/your-backend/pepperstone-webtrader.png" caption="Pepperstone Webtrader with the bottom panel showing the Positions, Orders, and Trade History tabs, and the right-click menu open on a position with Close and Modify." />

## Your trading history

- The **Trade History** tab holds your complete record of past trades, going back much further than the chart's day or two. It's the place to look when you want to review how you've done.

## Use it on your phone

- You can do all of this from the **Pepperstone app** too. Download it from the **Google Play Store** (Android) or the **App Store** (iPhone), sign in with the same details, and the Positions, Orders and History tabs are all there.

If the screen ever breaks mid-trade, you're not stuck watching it. Log in here, find the position, and close or adjust it — the same trade, reached a different way, which is the whole point of knowing where it is before you ever need it.
