## One account, two platforms

TradingView and your trading terminal are two ways into the **same** account — a trade you place on one shows up on the other, so you're never splitting your money in two.

- **TradingView**
  - Place and manage your trades right on the chart, where the Navigator's lines and signals are — you trade what you see.
  - It won't show the full instrument information (leverage, swap) before you trade, and it only keeps a day or two of history.
- **Your trading terminal (Pepperstone Webtrader or cTrader)**
  - A full trading platform in its own right — you can place and manage trades here too.
  - Look up the full instrument information for any market, and see your complete trade history.
  - A few extra tools the chart doesn't have, like a trailing stop.
  - Your backup way in if TradingView is ever down.
