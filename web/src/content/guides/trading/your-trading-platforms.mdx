## One account, two doors

You have one Pepperstone account, and two ways into it. **TradingView** is the chart, where the Navigator draws its lines and where you press Buy or Sell. Your **trading terminal** is the full platform behind it. A trade you place on one shows up on the other, because it's the same account and the same money.

<GuidePlatformMap />

## Which one, when?

- **Day to day, trade on TradingView.** The Navigator's lines and signals are there, so you trade what you see.
- **Open the terminal for the details.** TradingView won't show you an instrument's leverage or swap before you trade, and it only keeps a day or two of history. The terminal shows all of that, plus a few extras like a trailing stop.
- **If TradingView is ever down,** the terminal is your way in. You can place and manage trades there too.
