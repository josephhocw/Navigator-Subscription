This is the one that matters: your first trade, from open to close, right on the chart. The same steps work for everyone. Take it slowly, start small, and you'll have nothing to undo.

<GuideChecks title="What you'll need">
- A funded trading account connected to TradingView (See [the connect step](/guides/connect-pepperstone-tradingview#connect-to-pepperstone))
- Funds in your account (See [the funding step](/guides/open-pepperstone-free-tradingview#add-funds) on how to deposit)
</GuideChecks>

## On your computer

- **Check you're connected.** Your Pepperstone account name and balance show at the bottom of the chart. If not, go back to [the connect step](/guides/connect-pepperstone-tradingview).

<GuideShot maxHeight="150px" src="/guides/step-6/account-connected.png" caption="Your connected Pepperstone account shows in the panel at the bottom of the chart." />

- Under **Symbol Search**, click the white **Pepperstone** icon.

<GuideShot maxHeight="240px" src="/guides/step-6/symbol-search-pepperstone.png" caption="In Symbol Search, click the white Pepperstone icon." />

- Now when you search for any instrument, they'll only show **Pepperstone** charts — and you can trade on them.

<GuideShot narrow src="/guides/step-6/pepperstone-only-results.png" caption="Now every result is a Pepperstone chart you can trade." />

- **Click Buy or Sell.** The trading panel opens on the right. Choose your order type: **Market** (buy or sell now), **Limit** (at your price or better), or **Stop** (once price passes your level).

<GuideShot narrow src="/guides/step-6/order-types.png" caption="The order-type tabs at the top of the trading panel: Market, Limit, or Stop." />

Then set the order up in the same panel, top to bottom:

- **Place it.** Press the Buy or Sell button to confirm. Your open trade then shows in the positions panel at the bottom of the chart — ready to manage.

- **Two ways to manage your trades.** Either one works any time the market is open.
  - **On the chart.** Your entry, take-profit and stop-loss each show up as their own line. To move one, drag the line up or down. To cancel a pending order or close an open trade, click the ✕ on its line.

    <GuideShot src="/guides/step-6/manage-on-chart.png" caption="Your entry, take-profit and stop-loss each sit on the chart as a line you can drag." />

  - **In the trading panel at the bottom.** This is the same panel your trade appeared in. Each position shows its take-profit, stop-loss, profit, used margin, commission and swap. Use the pencil to change a trade or the ✕ to close it. The tabs along the top — Positions, Orders, Order history, Account summary — are also where you check your past trades and what each one cost.

    <GuideShot src="/guides/step-6/positions-panel.png" caption="The trading panel at the bottom: the pencil modifies a trade, the ✕ closes it, and the tabs hold your history." />

- **Tidy the chart if it gets busy.** Don't want the trade lines and labels on your chart? Right-click the chart → **Settings** → **Trading**, then untick **Positions and orders**. Unticking **Execution marks** as well clears the leftover markers and keeps the view clean.

<GuideShot narrow maxHeight="430px" src="/guides/step-6/chart-trading-settings.png" caption="Right-click the chart → Settings → Trading. Untick Positions and orders, and Execution marks too." />

- **If TradingView ever has a problem,** you can manage the same trade from [your trading terminal](/guides/trading/your-trading-terminal) instead.

## On your phone

- The flow is the same in the app. Open the chart and check your account is connected at the bottom.
- Open the order panel: on iPhone tap the **Buy/Sell** buttons, on Android tap the **two-arrows** symbol.
- Choose **Market**, set the minimum size, and set your take-profit and stop-loss in the same panel before you place.
- Your open trade shows in the positions panel, and you can modify or close it there too.

## Two things the chart won't show you

The chart is built for trading, not for digging up detail. Two things it can't do — and both live on your trading terminal:

- **The full instrument information** — leverage, swap and commission — *before* you place a trade. The chart shows you the lot size, not the rest.
- **More than a day or two of history.** For your complete record of past trades, you go to the broker.

That, plus a couple of extra tools and your backup way in, is what's next: [your trading terminal](/guides/trading/your-trading-terminal).

<GuideCallout title="Placing this trade unlocks your free 3 months of TradingView" tone="skip">
This is for anyone who hasn't claimed the free TradingView promotion yet. If you already have, skip this.

Placing your first trade unlocks the free 3 months. It can be a tiny minimum-lot trade, opened and closed just to cross the line. Place one small Market trade and the promotion is yours.
</GuideCallout>

<GuideCallout title="What if I mess it up, or lose money on my first trade?">
The downside is in your hands, set before you click.

Start at the minimum lot size, so any price move stays small, and set your stop-loss before you place the order, so the trade closes itself if it turns against you. Do those two and you've capped the worst case before the trade even opens.
</GuideCallout>
