This is the one that matters: your first trade, from open to close, right on the chart. The same steps work for everyone. Take it slowly, start small, and you'll have nothing to undo.

<GuideChecks title="What you'll need">
- Your Pepperstone account connected to TradingView (the first section below shows you how)
- Funds in your account (See [the funding step](/guides/open-pepperstone-free-tradingview#add-funds) on how to deposit)
</GuideChecks>

## Connect Pepperstone to TradingView

This is the quickest part — about two minutes, and you only do it once. It gives you live prices on your charts and Buy and Sell buttons that work straight from the chart.

- Log in at [tradingview.com](https://www.tradingview.com/). Click **Products** in the top menu, then **Supercharts**.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-4/open-supercharts.png" caption="Open the Products menu and choose Supercharts." />

- On the chart screen, click **Trade** at the top-right, then find **Pepperstone** in the list of brokers and click **Connect**.

<GuideFlow fill shots={[
  { src: '/guides/step-4/click-trade.png', caption: 'Click Trade at the top-right of the chart.' },
  { src: '/guides/step-4/connect-broker.png', caption: 'The Pepperstone tile in the broker list.' },
]} />

- Log in with your Pepperstone details and click **Approve Access**.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-4/approve-access.png" caption="Confirm the account, then click Approve access." maxHeight="500px" />

- Done — your Pepperstone account name now shows at the top-right of the chart.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-4/account-connected.png" caption="Pepperstone is connected — your live account now shows on the chart." />

- **On your phone (optional):** download the **TradingView** app from the App Store or Play Store, log in, open a chart and connect to Pepperstone the same way.

## On your computer

- **Check you're connected.** Your Pepperstone account name and balance show at the bottom of the chart. If not, go back to [Connect Pepperstone to TradingView](#connect-pepperstone-to-tradingview) above.

<GuideShot maxHeight="150px" src="/guides/step-6/account-connected.png" caption="Your connected Pepperstone account shows in the panel at the bottom of the chart." />

- Under **Symbol Search**, click the white **Pepperstone** icon.

<GuideShot maxHeight="240px" src="/guides/step-6/symbol-search-pepperstone.png" caption="In Symbol Search, click the white Pepperstone icon." />

- Now when you search for any instrument, they'll only show **Pepperstone** charts — and you can trade on them.

<GuideShot narrow src="/guides/step-6/pepperstone-only-results.png" caption="Now every result is a Pepperstone chart you can trade." />

- **Click Buy or Sell.** The trading panel opens on the right. Choose your order type: **Market** (buy or sell now), **Limit** (at your price or better), or **Stop** (once price passes your level).

<GuideShot narrow src="/guides/step-6/order-types.png" caption="The order-type tabs at the top of the trading panel: Market, Limit, or Stop." />

Then set the order up in the same panel, top to bottom:

<GuidePanelSteps
  src="/guides/step-6/order-panel.png"
  caption="Set your size, then your take-profit and stop-loss, and check the lot size before you place."
  imgWidth="300px"
  weights={[44, 12, 40, 22]}

    { lead: "Check your lot size.", body: "The <strong>Order info</strong> line at the bottom confirms the lot size you're about to trade." },
  ]}
/>

- **Place it.** Press the Buy or Sell button to confirm. Your open trade then shows in the positions panel at the bottom of the chart — ready to manage.

- **Two ways to manage your trades.** Either one works any time the market is open.
  - **On the chart.** Your entry, take-profit and stop-loss each show up as their own line. To move one, drag the line up or down. To cancel a pending order or close an open trade, click the ✕ on its line.

    <GuideShot src="/guides/step-6/manage-on-chart.png" caption="Your entry, take-profit and stop-loss each sit on the chart as a line you can drag." />

  - **In the trading panel at the bottom.** This is the same panel your trade appeared in. Each position shows its take-profit, stop-loss, profit, used margin, commission and swap. Use the pencil to change a trade or the ✕ to close it. The tabs along the top — Positions, Orders, Order history, Account summary — are also where you check your past trades and what each one cost.

    <GuideShot src="/guides/step-6/positions-panel.png" caption="The trading panel at the bottom: the pencil modifies a trade, the ✕ closes it, and the tabs hold your history." />

- **Tidy the chart if it gets busy.** Don't want the trade lines and labels on your chart? Right-click the chart → **Settings** → **Trading**, then untick **Positions and orders**. Unticking **Execution marks** as well clears the leftover markers and keeps the view clean.

<GuideShot narrow maxHeight="430px" src="/guides/step-6/chart-trading-settings.png" caption="Right-click the chart → Settings → Trading. Untick Positions and orders, and Execution marks too." />

- **If TradingView ever has a problem,** you can manage the same trade from [your trading terminal](/guides/trading/your-trading-terminal) instead.

## On your phone

- Download the **TradingView** app from the App Store or Play Store and log in.
- Open the chart and connect to Pepperstone.

<GuideFlow maxHeight="400px" align="left" shots={[
  { src: '/guides/step-4/phone-open-chart.png' },
  { src: '/guides/step-4/phone-connect-icon.png' },
  { src: '/guides/step-4/phone-select-pepperstone.png' }
]} />

- **Access the trading panel.**
  - **iPhone:** tap the **Buy/Sell** buttons at the top.
  - **Android:** tap the **two-arrows** symbol at the bottom.

The panel opens with the same fields as on computer — set your size, your take-profit and stop-loss, then tap **Buy** or **Sell** to place.

<GuideFlow maxHeight="400px" align="left" shots={[
  { src: '/guides/step-6/phone-access-trading-panel.png' },
  { src: '/guides/step-6/phone-order-panel.png' }
]} />

- **Monitor your trading positions.** Tap the **Pepperstone** icon at the bottom to open the positions panel — your balance, open trades and history all live here.

<GuideFlow maxHeight="400px" align="left" shots={[
  { src: '/guides/step-6/phone-positions-icon.png' },
  { src: '/guides/step-6/phone-positions-panel.png' }
]} />

That's a trade on the chart, from open to close. The last step is [your trading terminal](/guides/trading/your-trading-terminal) — the full platform behind your account, and your backup way in if the chart is ever down.
