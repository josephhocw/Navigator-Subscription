Our Telegram groups already send signals for the instruments on our list, so this step is optional. Alerts are for going beyond that — any instrument in your plan, on any timeframe you choose, delivered by TradingView itself.

## Create an alert

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-7/alert-dialog.png" caption="The alert window with RHO Navigator chosen as the condition." />

1. Make sure the Navigator is attached and the channel lines are showing ([Step 5](/guides/attach-the-navigator)).
2. Right-click the chart and choose **Add alert on [instrument]**.
3. Under **Condition**, select **RHO Navigator**. In the dropdown below it, select **Any alert() function call**.
4. Choose the timeframe you want the alert to watch.
5. Give the alert a name you'll recognise, if you like.
6. On the **Notifications** tab, tick **Notify in app** for a push to your phone, and **Send email** if you want it in your inbox too.
7. Click **Create**. Check the alert shows a green **Active** status in the alerts panel (the alarm-clock icon on the right).

## Manage your alerts

You can stop, edit, or delete alerts any time from that same panel.

### How many alerts can I have?

TradingView sets the limits by plan: the free plan has none, Essential gets 20, Plus gets 100, and Premium gets 400. One instrument's alert counts as one.

On Premium there's a shortcut worth knowing: click a watchlist's title, choose **Add alert on the list**, and one alert covers every instrument in it — all on the same timeframe.

<GuideCallout title="After we update the Navigator">
Your existing alerts keep running on the old version — they don't update themselves. After an update, **delete your alerts and create them again**. It takes a minute and makes sure your alerts match what's on your chart.
</GuideCallout>
