Every subscription comes with a private Telegram signal group for each market in your plan. This page explains what lands in that group, how the signals are made, and the right way to use them.

## The two types of signal

Two kinds of signal reach you on Telegram, and they live in two different places.

<GuideSignalTypes />

## How the Navigator signals are generated

- **Every Navigator signal comes from the 15-minute timeframe**, using Pepperstone chart data. To see a signal line up on your own chart, switch your chart to 15m.

  <GuideShot src="/learn/signals/chart-15m-pepperstone.png" caption="A chart that matches the signals: the instrument bar reads 15 · Pepperstone, with 15m selected in the timeframe row." alt="The TradingView instrument bar for NAS100 reading US 100 Cash CFD, 15, Pepperstone, with the 15m timeframe selected above" />

- **Every label fires a signal except two:** the middle support/resistance dots and the grey **NC** (new channel) label. Support and resistance hits, buy and sell signals, breakouts — they all reach the group.

That Pepperstone detail matters more than it sounds. A different broker's price feed produces slightly different bars, which means different channels — and the signals won't match what you see. This is why we ask subscribers to chart on Pepperstone data.

## The three groups of signal

Every message the bot sends belongs to one of three groups, and the group tells you what the message is asking of you: a look, a trade, or caution.

<GuideSignalGroups />

## Use them properly

**The [core skills](/guides/master/core-skills) always come first.** When it comes to entries, read the channel yourself — a signal or label is a prompt to look, never the entry itself.

And mute the instrument topics you don't trade — around 10 instruments per market adds up to a lot of notifications. Press and hold a topic to mute it.

<GuideShot narrow maxHeight="450px" capWidth="220px" src="/learn/signals/navigator-signals-group.png" caption="Each instrument is its own topic. Press and hold one to mute it." />

## Looking back at older signals

Signals belong to the channel that was live when they fired. To review the signals from a previous channel, use TradingView's Bar Replay to step back to the bar just before the **NC** (new channel) marker — the chart redraws the old channel and its labels. The steps are in [Understand the features](/guides/master/read-the-chart).

## Want alerts for other instruments?

Our signal list covers the popular instruments in each market. For anything else, you can build your own alerts in TradingView — sent to your phone or email, on any timeframe you choose. The walkthrough is the next stage, [Set your alerts](/guides/master/set-your-alerts).
