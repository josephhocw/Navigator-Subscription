## How alerts reach you

TradingView delivers each alert two ways — a notification on your phone through the TradingView app, and an email to your inbox. You choose one or both when you create the alert; either way it carries the full signal, with the price and the take-profit and stop-loss levels.

## How many alerts can you have?

TradingView counts alerts by instrument: an alert on Gold (XAUUSD) is one technical alert, an alert on EURUSD is another. How many you can run at once depends on your TradingView plan:

The free Basic plan can't create any — one more reason the free TradingView Premium through Pepperstone is worth claiming. On Premium, 400 is more than you'll ever need, and there's a shortcut worth knowing: click a watchlist's title, choose **Add alert on the list**, and one alert covers every instrument in it, all on the same timeframe.

## Create an alert

- Make sure the Navigator is attached and the channel lines are showing ([Step 5](/guides/attach-the-navigator)).
- Right-click anywhere on the chart and choose **Add alert on [instrument]** — the name matches whatever chart you're on.

## Manage your alerts

- Click **Alerts** — the alarm-clock icon in the panel on the right of the chart.

<GuideShot narrow maxHeight="300px" src="/learn/alerts/alerts-panel-icon.png" caption="The alarm-clock icon on the right opens your alerts panel." />

- Hover over any alert to stop or resume it, change its settings, or delete it.

<GuideShot narrow maxHeight="200px" src="/learn/alerts/alert-controls.png" caption="Left to right: pause or resume, settings, delete." />

### What the statuses mean

<GuideMediaCard src="/learn/alerts/alert-statuses.png" alt="The TradingView alerts panel showing four alerts with different statuses: Stopped — Triggered, Stopped — Expired, Stopped manually, and Active" imgWidth="46%">
- <strong style="color:#26a69a">Active</strong> — running and watching.
- <strong style="color:#f7a600">Stopped — Triggered</strong> — an alert set to fire "Only once" has fired.
- <strong style="color:#a3b1c6">Stopped manually</strong> — you paused it yourself.
- <strong style="color:#ef5350">Stopped — Expired</strong> — it reached its expiry date.
</GuideMediaCard>

<GuideCallout tone="skip" title="After every Navigator update: delete and recreate your alerts">
Alerts don't follow the Navigator when we update it. An existing alert keeps running on the old version it was created with, so what it sends you can stop matching what's on your chart. Whenever we announce an update, delete your alerts (the bin icon, then **Delete**) and create them again. It takes a minute.

<GuideShot narrow maxHeight="170px" src="/learn/alerts/delete-alert-confirm.png" caption="Deleting is two clicks: the bin icon, then Delete." />
</GuideCallout>
