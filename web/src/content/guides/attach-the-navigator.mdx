## Choose your instrument

Click **Symbol Search** at the top-left. Click the white **Pepperstone** button in the filter row so you only see Pepperstone charts — those are the ones you can actually trade on. A TSLA chart from another source will display fine, but the Buy and Sell buttons won't work on it.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-5/symbol-search-filter.png" caption="Symbol Search with the Pepperstone filter on." />

## Set your timeframe

Pick an interval from the top panel — our Telegram signals use **15m**. Click the star next to the timeframes you use often and they'll sit in the top panel for one-click access.

## Attach the indicator

Click **Indicators, metrics, and strategies** in the top panel, then the **Invite-only** tab on the left. Find **RHO Navigator** with your market in the brackets and click it once.

Loading takes 10 to 20 seconds — you'll see three dots at the top-left while it works. The trendlines and panels appear on their own once it's done.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-5/navigator-attached.png" caption="The Navigator on a chart — channel lines, labels and panels." />

## Do the same on your phone

1. Download the **TradingView** app from the App Store or Play Store and log in.
2. Open a chart and connect to Pepperstone, same as on the computer.
3. Tap **Indicators**, then **Invite-Only**, then **RHO Navigator** with your market in the brackets.
4. If the channel lines don't show, swipe the chart to the right until the channel's starting point is visible.
5. Pinch the chart or the price scale with two fingers to resize. If the panels are in the way, untick the two panel boxes in the indicator's settings and tap **OK**.

## Fix the common hiccups

- **Labels show but no channel lines.** Scroll the chart to the left, or zoom out (hold **Ctrl** and scroll back on your mouse). The lines only draw once the channel's starting point is on screen — a TradingView quirk, not a fault.
- **An exclamation mark on the indicator.** The chart is a market that isn't in your plan. Switch to one of your markets.
- **Panels covering the chart.** Open the indicator's **Settings** and untick the top and bottom panel boxes. It takes a moment to redraw.
- **Updating the indicator.** Hover over the indicator's name on the chart and click **Update** if it appears. No update button usually means you're already on the latest — to be certain, remove the indicator and attach it again.
- **Using several tabs.** You can open TradingView in multiple tabs with the Navigator on different instruments, but only one tab connects to Pepperstone — place all your trades from that tab.
