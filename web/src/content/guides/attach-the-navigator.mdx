Once we've granted your access (by 12:00 noon the day after you subscribe), the Navigator appears in your TradingView indicator list, ready to attach to any chart in your plan.

## Choose your instrument

- Click **Symbol Search** at the top-left.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-5/symbol-search-button.png" caption="Click the symbol box at the top-left to open Symbol Search." maxHeight="150px" />

- Click the white **Pepperstone** button in the filter row, so you only see Pepperstone charts — the ones you can actually trade on.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-5/symbol-search-filter.png" caption="Symbol Search with the Pepperstone filter on." maxHeight="240px" />

- A chart from another source will display fine, but its **Buy** and **Sell** buttons won't work. Stick to the Pepperstone charts.

## Set your timeframe

- Click 'Chart interval' to choose from different timeframes.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-5/chart-interval.png" caption="Click the Chart interval arrow to pick a timeframe." maxHeight="90px" />

- Click the **star** next to the timeframes you use often, and they'll sit in the top panel for one-click access.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-5/star-timeframe.png" caption="Click the star to add a timeframe to your favourites." maxHeight="320px" />

## Attach the indicator

- Click **Indicators, metrics, and strategies** in the top panel.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-5/indicators-button.png" caption="Click Indicators in the top panel." maxHeight="155px" />

- Click on the **Invite-only** tab on the left and left click on the **RHO Navigator [your market]**.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-5/invite-only-list.png" caption="Open the Invite-only tab and pick the RHO Navigator for your market." maxHeight="300px" />

- It takes 10 to 20 seconds to load — you'll see three dots at the top-left while it works.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-5/loading-dots.png" caption="The three dots at the top-left mean the Navigator is still loading." maxHeight="210px" />

- Once the indicator has finished loading, you should see the channel lines and labels appear, like this.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-5/navigator-attached.png" caption="The Navigator fully loaded — channel lines, labels and the trend panel." />

- If you see labels but no channel lines (like the image below), scroll the chart to the left, or zoom out (hold **Ctrl** and scroll back on your mouse). The lines only draw once the channel's starting point is on screen — a TradingView quirk, not a fault.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-5/no-channel-lines.png" caption="Labels show but no channel lines yet — scroll left or zoom out to bring the channel's start into view." />

<GuideCallout title="Pro tip: open several charts at once">
You can open TradingView in several tabs, each with the Navigator on a different instrument. But only one tab can connect to Pepperstone for trading, so only place your trades on that particular tab.

<GuideShot src="/guides/step-5/multiple-tabs.png" caption="A few TradingView tabs open on different instruments." maxHeight="90px" />
</GuideCallout>

## Do the same on your phone

- Download the **TradingView** app from the App Store or Play Store and log in.
- Open the chart and connect to Pepperstone.

- Click **Indicators**, then **Invite-Only** and **RHO Navigator [your market]**.

<GuideFlow width="175px" shots={[

- If the channel lines don't show, swipe the chart to the right until the channel's starting point is visible.
- Pinch the chart or the price scale with two fingers to resize. If the panels are in the way, untick the two panel boxes in the indicator's settings and tap **OK**.

## Fix the common hiccups

- **An exclamation mark on the indicator.** The chart is a market that isn't in your plan. Switch to one of your markets.
- **Panels covering the chart.** Open the indicator's **Settings** and untick the top and bottom panel boxes. It takes a moment to redraw.
- **Updating the indicator.** Hover over the indicator's name on the chart and click **Update** if it appears. No update button usually means you're already on the latest — to be certain, remove the indicator and attach it again.
