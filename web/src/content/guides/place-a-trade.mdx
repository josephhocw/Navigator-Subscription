## On your computer

- **Check you're connected.** Your Pepperstone account name and balance show at the top of the chart. If not, go back to [Step 4](/guides/connect-pepperstone-tradingview).
- **Check the chart is tradable.** Use **Symbol Search** with the white **Pepperstone** filter on — only Pepperstone charts can be traded.
- **Click Buy or Sell.** The trading panel opens on the right. Choose your order type: **Market** (buy or sell now), **Limit** (at your price or better), or **Stop** (once price passes your level).
- **Set your size.** Not sure of the minimum? Clear the lot-size box and it shows you. Do watch the size on some instruments — Gold, for one, trades in lots of 100.
- **Set your protection.** You can set a take-profit and stop-loss in the same panel before placing the order.
- **Place it**, then keep an eye on it in the positions panel at the bottom.

## On your phone

- Same flow in the app: open the chart and make sure Pepperstone is connected.
- Open the trading panel — on iPhone it's the **Buy/Sell** buttons, on Android the **two-arrows** symbol.
- Your open positions show in the same panel.
